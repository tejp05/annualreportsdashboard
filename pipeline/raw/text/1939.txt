T o THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
Your directors submit for the information of Stock-
holders Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for the 
year 1939, together with Consolidated Balance Sheet as 
of December 31, 1939, with the Auditors' Certificate 
attached. In addition to the usual Consolidated Balance 
Sheet of the domestic companies and the Canadian sub-
sidiary company, as published in previous years, there is 
submitted herewith a Consolidated Balance Sheet of the 
domestic companies. 
Net Profit for the year 1939, including foreign sub-
sidiaries and branches, but before deducting Federal 
Taxes, was $11,362,692.27, compared with $10,830,033.86 
for the year 1938, being an increase of $532,658.41. As in 
the previous year, a reserve for the full amount of the 
Blocked Foreign Net Profits has been deducted in arriv-
ing at the Net Profit for the year 1939 above reported. 
After deductingestimated Federal Taxesof $2,270,000.00, 
the Net Profit for the year 1939 was $9,092,692.27, com-
pared with $8,660,033.86 for the year 1938, being an in-


===== PAGE 6 =====
crease of $432,658.41. The Net Profit for 1939 equals 
$10.63 per share on the 855,408 shares outstanding Decem-
ber 31, 1939, compared with $10.12 per share for the 
year 1938 if computed on the same number of shares, 
being an increase of $.51 per share. A stock dividend of 
40,734 shares was issued April 1, 1939. The Net Profit 
for 1938 on the 814,674 shares actually outstanding at the 
end of that year was $10.63 per share. 
A cash dividend of $150,000.00 was received in Decem-
ber 1939 on the Company's holding of 100,000 shares of 
the Class "B" capital stock of the Hobart Manufacturing 
Company. 
An amount of $1,193,327.89 was transferred from Sur-
plus to Reserve for Contingencies, which Reserve, as of 
December 31, 1939, amounts to $3,000,000.00. Also an 
amount of $2,000,000.00 was transferred from Surplus to 
write Patents and Goodwill Account down by a like 
amount. 
There was invested during 1939 $7,548,360.62 in rental 
machines, plant and equipment and land and buildings. 
Dismantled and obsolete equipment amounting to $3,566,-
282.41 was written off and charged against reserves 
provided out of prior and current years' earnings. 
Four quarterly cash dividends of $1.50 per share were 
paid during the year. In addition, a stock dividend of

To the Stockholders of the 
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION: 
We have made an examination of the Consolidated Balance Sheet 
of the International Business Machines Corporation and Domestic 
and Canadian Subsidiary Companies as at December 31, 1939, the 
Consolidated Balance Sheet of the International Business Machines 
Corporation and Domestic Subsidiary Companies as at the same 
date, and of the Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for the 
year ended on that date. In connection therewith, we have examined 
or tested accounting records of the Domestic Companies, and other 
supporting evidence, and have also made a general review of the 
accounting methods and the operating accounts for the period, but 
we have not made a detailed audit of all transactions. The Com-
panies' accounts have been maintained in accordance with accepted 
accounting principles on a basis consistent with the preceding year, 
except for the modification of the method of converting foreign 
currencies. 
We have accepted, after review, a report of independent auditors 
on the accounts of the Canadian corporation and the Company's 
latest available financial statements, supplemented by cabled in-
formation, on the accounts of the other foreign subsidiaries and 
branches. 
In our opinion, the accompanying Consolidated Balance Sheet of 
the International Business Machines Corporation and Domestic and 
Canadian Subsidiary Companies, the Consolidated Balance Sheet of 
the International Business Machines Corporation and Domestic 
Subsidiary Companies, and the related Consolidated Income and 
Surplus Account fairly present the financial condition of the Company 
and its Domestic and Canadian Subsidiaries and of the Company and 
its Domestic Subsidiaries as at December 31, 1939 and the results 
of operations for the year ended on that date. 
R. G. RANKIN & CO. 
Certified Public Accountants
