T o THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
Your directors submit herewith income and surplus account for the 
year 1925, together with consolidated balance sheet as of December 31, 
1925, with auditors' certificate attached. 
The net income for the year, before Federal Taxes, but after provid-
ing for full depreciation ($1,055,585.62), and development and patent 
expenses ($353,988.27), was $3,203,532.66, compared with $2,425,337.36, 
for the year 1924, being an increase of $778,195.30. 
Net current assets totaled $8,256,768.53, compared with $6,811,-
288.33, at the close of 1924, an increase of $1,445,480.20, the current 
assets being over five times the current Habilities. 
During the year the company retired and cancelled bonds of a par 
value of $94,000 and also acquired $28,000 par value of additional bonds 
which are held in the treasury. 
In June, 1925, 10,046 additional shares of capital stock were sold 
to the stockholders at $100 per share. 
In December, 1925, a stock 
dividend of 20% was paid, amounting to 32,147 shares. 
The result of 
these two issues was to increase the capital stock outstanding at the 
close of the year to 192,881 shares. The dividend rate of $2.00 quarterly 
was maintained on the increased shares. 
By order of the Board of Directors,
