To the Stockholders of the 
COMPUTING-TABULATING-RECORDING-CO. 
We are submitting herewith Statement of Earnings for the 
year 1914 and Consolidated Balance Sheet as of December 31, 
1914, certified by The Audit Company of New York. 
While this statement does not show the customary increase, 
we regard it as highly satisfactory in view of business conditions. 
Our European income was cut off as of July 1, 1914, and the 
resulting conditions in this country were responsible for a con-
siderable decrease in gross business, especially during the latter 
part of 1914, as compared with the previous year. 
It will be noted that our bonded indebtedness has been re-
duced during the year by $158,500 and our notes and accounts 
payable diminished by nearly $150,000. The item of $76,947.83 
deducted from surplus covers finished stock and parts on hand 
which have become obsolete. 
Our organization is growing stronger every day and there is 
every reason to expect a substantial increase in all of our lines as 
general business conditions improve. 
By order of the BOARD OF DIRECTORS. 
GEORGE W . FAIRCHILD, Chairman. 
New York, March 2,1915.
