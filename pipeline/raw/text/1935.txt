T o THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
Your directors submit for the information of Stockholders 
ConsoHdated Income and Surplus Account for the year 1935, 
together with Consolidated Balance Sheet as at December 
31, 1935, with the Auditors' Certificate attached. 
Net Income for the year 1935, including Foreign Sub-
sidiaries and Branches, before deducting Federal Taxes, but 
after providing for full Depreciation ($2,976,605.31), De-
velopment and Patent Expense ($1,012,805.48) and Interest 
($131,881.20) was $8,127,530.69, compared with $7,522,-
362.55 for the year 1934, being an increase of $605,168.14. 
After deducting estimated Federal Taxes of $1,037,000.00, 
the Net Income for the year was $7,090,530.69, equal to 
$9.88 per share on 717,412 shares outstanding December 31, 
1935, compared with $6,597,362.55, equal to $9.19 per share, 
for the year 1934, based on an equal number of shares, 
being an increase of 69 cents per share. The Net Income for 
1934, based on 703,345 shares outstanding at the end of 
that year, was $9.38 per share. Pursuant to rulings of the 
Internal Revenue Department, various rates of depre-
ciation heretofore used on certain plant and equipment have 
been changed in 1935. Less than $11,000. of the above 
increased profit is due to such change. 


===== PAGE 6 =====
The above Income includes a cash dividend of $125,000.00 
received in December 1935 on the company's holding of 
100,000 shares of the Class "B" capital stock of the Hobart 
Manufacturing Company. 
Net Current Assets at the end of the year were $2,665,-
676.59, compared with $2,970,761.26 at the end of 1934. 
Expenditures totalling $7,270,875.00 were made during 
the year for rental machines, plant and equipment and land 
and buildings. A total of $3,205,228.00 of dismantled and 
obsolete equipment was written off and charged against 
reserves. 
The mortgage of $187,500.00 on our property at 310 Fifth 
Avenue, New York, became due and was paid on July 1, 
1935. 
The Company includes in its consolidated earnings the 
undistributed earnings of its foreign subsidiaries. Due to 
difficulty in getting remittances from certain foreign coun-
tries, owing to foreign governmental exchange restrictions, a 
reserve has been set aside in the amount of $722,015.77, the 
full amount of the blocked net profits. 
Regular quarterly cash dividends of $1.50 per share were 
paid during the year and in addition a stock dividend of 
3 per cent was declared in November 1935, payable February 
10, 1936, to stockholders of record December 20, 1935. The 
necessary additional 21,522 shares, including scrip for frac-
tional shares, have been issued accordingly. The sum of

To the Stockholders of the 
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION: 
We have made an examination of the accounts and records relating 
to the consolidated balance sheet of the International Business Machines 
Corporation and its Domestic Subsidiary as at December 31, 1935 and 
reviewed the Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for the year 
ended on that date, but we have not made a detailed audit of all trans-
actions. In that connection, we accepted after review an independent 
Auditor's Report on the accounts of the Canadian Corporation and the 
Company's own financial statements on the accounts of other Foreign 
Subsidiaries and Branches. The Company's accounts have been con-
sistently maintained in accordance with accepted accounting principles 
and all information and explanations desired were furnished. 
The accompanying consolidated balance sheet, based upon such 
examination, fairly presents, in our opinion, the financial condition of 
the International Business Machines Corporation and its Domestic 
and Canadian Subsidiary Companies as at December 31, 1935, and the 
related consolidated income and surplus account the results of opera-
tions for the year ended on that date. 
R. G. RANKIN & CO. 
Certified Public Accountants 
February 27, 1936
