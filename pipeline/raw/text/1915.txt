TO THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
We present, herewith, Consolidated Income Account of the 
Computing-Tabulating-Recording-Co. and Subsidiary Companies for 
the fiscal year ending December 31, 1915, together with a Con-
solidated Balance Sheet as of the same date. 
It will be noted that the financial condition of the Company is 
very satisfactory. 
Annual sinking fund requirements have been com-
plied with, and bonded indebtedness thereby reduced $152,000.00. 
Net 
current assets show a substantial 
gain over any 
previous 
statement. 
The net earnings for the year, after deducting for maintenance, 
depreciation, all expenses and bond interest, amount to $690,693.96, 
as compared with $189,861.59 for the previous year,—an increase of 
$290,832.37. 
We consider these results gratifying, in view of 
the 
fact that the first half of 1915 was considerably behind the same 
period for 1914. 
The steady improvement in our business, 
which 
started last July, continues, and we anticipate further gains this year. 
We are using every effort to further the development of new 
and improved devices which, when completed, we believe, will have a 
favorable effect on future earnings. 
We feel that substantial pro-
gress is being made along this line. 
At a meeting of the Directors, on February 24, 1916, a dividend 
of 4% was declared, payable quarterly, as follows: 
1% on April 10, 
1916, to stockholders of record at the close of business on March 25, 
1916; 1% on July 10, 1916, to stockholders of record at the close of 
business on June 24, 1916; I96 on October 10, 1916, to stockholders 
of record at the close of business on September 25, 1916, and l^c on 
January 10, 1917, to stockholders of record at the close of business 
on December 23, 1916. 
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, 
THOS. J. WATSON, President

To the Board of Directors of the 
C O M P U T I N G - T A B U L A T I N G - R E C O R D I N G - C O M P A N Y , 
New York, N. Y. 
We have audited the books and accounts of the Computing-
Tabulating-Recording-Company and of its Subsidiary Companies for 
the year ending December 31, 1915, and certify that the attached 
Consolidated Balance Sheet is correctly prepared from the acijounts, 
and in our opinion properly sets forth the financial position of the 
Company and its Subsidiary Companies at December 
31 1915, and 
that the Consolidated Income Account correctly states the Profits 
for the year ending on that date. 
Income and Expenditure for the year have been verified; all 
capitalized expenditures have been examined; and we have satisfied 
ourselves that adequate provision has been made for Depreciation 
of Plants and Equipment. 
Care has been taken, fully to state the 
Liabilities, and proper provision has been made for all known accruing 
expenses or losses. 
The Cash on hand at main offices. Bank Balances, and Invest-
ment Securities owned have been verified by actual count, inspection, 
or certification obtained from the Depositaries. 
Capital Stock and Bonds outstanding have been certified to us 
by the Registrars and Trustees. 
(Signed) 
A. S. FEDDE & CO., 
Certified Public Accountants. 
New York. February 24. 1916.
