T o THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
Your directors submit for the information of Stock-
holders Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for 
the year 1938, together with Consolidated Balance Sheet 
as of December 31, 1938, with the Auditors' Certificate 
attached. 
Net Profit for the year 1938, including foreign sub-
sidiaries and branches, but before deducting Federal 
Taxes, was $10,830,033.86, compared with $10,412,512.80 
for the year 1937, being an increase of $417,521.06. As 
in the previous year, a reserve for the full amount of the 
Blocked Foreign Net Profits has been deducted in ar-
riving at the Net Profit for the year 1938 above reported. 
After deducting estimated Federal Taxes of $2,170,-
000.00, the Net Profit for the year 1938 was $8,660,033.86, 
compared with $8,082,512.80 for the year 1937, being an 
increase of $577,521.06. The Net Profit for 1938 equals 
$10.63 per share on the 814,674 shares outstanding 


===== PAGE 6 =====
December 31, 1938, compared with $9.92 per share for 
the year 1937 if computed on the same number of shares, 
being an increase of $.71 per share. A stock dividend of 
38,794 shares was issued April 1, 1938. The Net Profit 
for 1937 on the 775,880 shares actually outstanding at 
the end of that year was $10.42 per share. 
A cash dividend of $150,000.00 was received in Decem-
ber 1938 on the Company's holding of 100,000 shares of 
the Class "B" capital stock of the Hobart Manufacturing 
Company. 
The amount of $500,000.00 was transferred from 
Surplus to Reserve for Contingencies as of Decem-
ber 31, 1938. 
There was invested during 1938 $10,343,328.41 in rent-
al machines, plant and equipment and land and build-
ings, Dismantled and obsolete equipment amounting to 
$4,681,821.71 was written off and charged against re-
serves provided out of prior and current years' earnings. 
Four quarterly cash dividends of $1.50 per share 
were paid during the year. In addition, a stock dividend 
of 5% (40,734 shares) was declared in November 1938,

To the Stockholders of the 
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION: 
We have made an examination of the Consolidated Balance Sheet 
of the International Business Machines Corporation and its Domestic 
and Canadian Subsidiaries as at December 31, 1938, and of the 
Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for the year ended on 
that date. In connection therewith, we have examined or tested 
accounting records of the Domestic Companies, and other supporting 
evidence, and have also made a general review of the accounting 
methods and of the operating and income accounts for the year, 
but we have not made a detailed audit of all transactions. The 
Companies' accounts have been consistently maintained in accordance 
with accepted accounting principles during the year under review 
and all information and explanations desired were furnished. 
We have accepted and included, after review, an independent 
auditor's report on the accounts of the Canadian corporation and 
the Company's own financial statements on the accounts of the 
other foreign subsidiaries and branches. 
In our opinion, based upon such examination, the accompanying 
Consolidated Balance Sheet fairly presents the financial condition 
of the International Business Machines Corporation and its Domestic 
and Canadian Subsidiary Companies as at December 31, 1938, and 
the related Consolidated Income and Surplus Account the results 
of operations for the year ended on that date. 
R. G. RANKIN & CO. 
Certified Public Accountants. 
February 24, 1939.
