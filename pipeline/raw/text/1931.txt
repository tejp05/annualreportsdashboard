T o THE STOCKHOLDERS: 
Your Directors submit herewith Consolidated Income and Surplus 
Account for the Year 1931, together with Consolidated Balance Sheet as 
of December 31, 1931, with the Auditors' certificate attached. 
The Net Income for the year, including Foreign Subsidiaries, before 
deducting Federal Taxes, but after providing for full Depreciation 
($1,928,842.82), Development and Patent Expenses ($752,544.76), Inter-
est ($171,526.08), and losses on Foreign Exchange ($38,318.01 actual 
and 
$274,900.76 
reserved 
for 
possible 
additional 
losses), 
was 
$8,222,386.30, compared with $8,157,816.52 for the Year 1930, being an 
increase of $64,569.78. 
After deducting estimated Federal Taxes, the Net Income was $11.08 
per share on 669,852 shares outstanding December 31, 1931, compared 
with $10.98 per share for the Year 1930, based on a like number of shares. 
The Net Income for 1930, based on 637,954 shares actually outstanding 
at the end of that year, was $11.53 per share. 
In November, 1931 a stock dividend of 5% was authorized. The addi-
tional 33,493 shares called for by this stock dividend were issued January 
11,1932. Regular cash dividends at the rate of $1.50 quarterly were paid 
during the year 1931. 
Net Current Assets at the end of the year amounted to $8,075,578.63, 
compared with $7,864,268.01 at the close of 1930. Cash and marketable 
securities at the end of the year totalled $4,885,145.48, compared with 
$4,367,503.84 at the close of 1930, being an increase of $517,641.64. 
The Company has continued the policy of retiring its bonds as and 
when surplus funds are available. During 1931 bonds of a total par 
value of $264,000.00 were retired and cancelled. In October, 1931 there 
was deposited with the Trustees of the Sinking Fund, cash for the redemp-
tion of an additional $736,000.00 par value of bonds called for payment 
January 1, 1932. These have since been retired and cancelled. Out of an 
original bond issue of $7,000,000.00, the Company has now retired a total 
of $5,012,000.00, leaving $1,988,000.00 in the hands of the public at 
March 1, 1932.

To the Stockholders of the 
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION: 
We have examined the accounts and records of the INTERNATIONAL 
BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION and its domestic and Canadian sub-
sidiary companies for the year ended December 31, 1931 and accepted 
after review the annual reports of other foreign subsidiary companies. 
We hereby certify that, in our opinion, the Consolidated Balance Sheet, 
as compiled from the accounts of the corporation, correctly reflects the 
financial condition of the INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES COR-
PORATION AND ITS DOMESTIC AND CANADIAN SUBSIDIARY COMPANIES as 
at December 31, 1981 and that the relative Summary of Consolidated 
Income reflects the Net Profit for the year ended on that date. 
THE AUDIT COMPANY OF NEW YORK 
R . G. RANKIN, 
President. 
New York, February 29, 1932.
