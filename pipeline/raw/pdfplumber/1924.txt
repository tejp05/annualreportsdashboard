To THE STOCKHOLDERS:
Your directors submit herewith income and surplus account for
the year 1924, together with consolidated balance sheet as of December
31, 1924, with auditors' certificate attached.
The net income for the year, before Federal Taxes, but after provid-
ing for full depreciation ($979,810.35), and development and patent
expenses ($315,659.86), was $2,425,337.36, compared with $2,161,375.17,
for the year 1923, being an increase of $263,962.19.
Net current assets totaled $6,811,288.33, compared with $6,723,
354.97, at the close of 1923, an increase of $87,933.36, the current assets
being over five times the current liabilities.
In February, 1924, the dividend rate was increased from $1.50 per
share quarterly to $2.00 per share quarterly.
During the year the company retired and cancelled bonds of a par
value of $103,500.
The factory at Sindelfingen, Germany, began production of goods
during 1924, and the Paris factory is expected to start production
during 1925.

To the Stockholders of the
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION:
We have made a general audit of the books and accounts of the
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION, and its Subsidiary-
Companies, for the year ending December 31, 1924.
We certify that the Consolidated Balance Sheet and Related Sum-
mary of Consolidated Income, Surplus and Declared Capital presented
herewith, are true Exhibits of the accounts and that, in our opinion,
they correctly set forth the financial condition of the INTERNATIONAL
BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION AND SUBSIDIARY COMPANIES as of
December 31,1924, and the results from operations for the period stated.
Very truly yours
THE AUDIT COMPANY OF NEW YORK
A. W. DUNNING, H. I. LUNDQUIST,
President. Secretary.
