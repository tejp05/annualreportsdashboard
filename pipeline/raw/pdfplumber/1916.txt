TO THE STOCKHOLDERS:
We present, herewith, Consolidated Income Account and Balance
Sheet of the Computing-Tabulating-Recording-Co. and its Subsidiary
Companies for the fiscal year ending December 31, 1916, with Auditor's
certificate attached.
The net earnings for the year, after deducting for maintenance,
depreciation, all expenses and bond interest, amount to $1,206,853.38,
as compared with $690,693.96 for the previous year—an increase of
$51(5,159.42.
Our annual sinking fund requirements have been complied with,
and bonded indebtedness thereby reduced $131,000. The increase of
$495,158.44 in inventories, which are taken at cost, or less, is due
both to the increased prices of material, and because it has been
thought advisable, on account of the raw material situation, to carry a
much larger stock than heretofore.
Our plants have been maintained in excellent physical condition,
although operations for some months past have been beyond their
normal capacity. To meet the heavy volume of business, we have
equipped a factory in Canada for the manufacture of Time Recorders,
and made several additions to our other plants, as well as large
purchases of machinery and equipment.
The business so far during 1917 shows a substantial gain over the
same period of 1916, and with our increased manufacturing facilities
and factory organization, together with new devices which are being
marketed for the first time and enlarged sales organization, we are
confident of satisfactory results.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
