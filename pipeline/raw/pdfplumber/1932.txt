To THE STOCKHOLDERS:
Your Directors submit herewith Consolidated Income and Surplus
Account for the Year 1932, together with Consolidated Balance Sheet as
of December 31, 1932, with the Auditors' certificate attached.
The Net Income for the year, including Foreign Subsidiaries, before
deducting Federal Taxes, but after providing for full Depreciation
($2,180,125.99), Development and Patent Expenses ($809,979.20),
Interest ($118,945.00), and losses on Foreign Exchange ($89,924.27
actual and $51,250.47 reserved for possible additional losses), was
$7,382,757.33, compared with $8,222,386.30 for the Year 1931, being a
decrease of $839,628.97.
After deducting estimated Federal Taxes, the Net Income was $9.11
per share on 703,345 shares outstanding December 31, 1932, compared
with $10.55 per share for the year 1931, based on a like number of shares.
The Net Income for 1931, based on 669,852 shares outstanding at the
end of that year, was $11.08 per share.
Net Current Assets at the end of the year amounted to $8,299,026.61
compared with $8,075,578.63 at the close of 1931. Cash and marketable
securities at the end of the year totalled $5,770,868.21, compared with
$4,885,145.48 at the dose of 1931, being an increase of $885,722.73.
During the Year 1932 the Company retired and cancelled bonds of a
total par value of $769,500.00, and in addition there was deposited in
November 1932 for Sinking Fund operation January 1, 1933, sufficient
funds for the redemption of $466,500.00 par value of bonds, leaving
$1,488,000.00 outstanding January 2, 1933 out of an original bond issue
of $7,000,000.00.
Your Directors established, as of December 31, 1932, a General Com-
pany Welfare Fund to be administered as may from time to time be
authorized by the-Board of Directors, and the 5,600 shares of Interna-
tional Business Machines Corporation stock held in the Treasury at that
date were transferred to such Welfare Fund at the net cost value of
$364,521.93.

To the Stockholders of the
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION:
We have examined the accounts and records of the INTERNATIONAL
BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION and its domestic and Canadian sub-
sidiary companies for the year ended December 31, 1932 and accepted
after review the annual reports of other foreign subsidiary companies.
We hereby certify that, in our opinion, the Consolidated Balance Sheet,
as compiled from the accounts of the corporation, correctly reflects the
financial condition of the INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES COR-
PORATION AND ITS DOMESTIC AND CANADIAN SUBSIDIARY COMPANIES as
at December 31, 1932 and that the relative Summary of Consolidated
Income reflects the Net Profit for the year ended on that date.
THE AUDIT COMPANY OF NEW YORK
R. G. RANKIN,
President.
