To the Stockholders of the
COMPUTING-TABULATING-RECORDING-COMPANY.
Herewith is submitted the audited Consolidated Balance Sheet of
the Computing-Tabulating-Recording-Company and Subsidiary Com-
panies, as of December 31, 1912, also the Statement of Earnings for the
year 1912, certified by The Audit Company of New York.
A conservative and more uniform system of accounting has been
put into effect, which is reflected in the substantial reserves shown in
the Consolidated Balance Sheet and Statement of Earnings.
The earnings of the Company steadily increased during the year,
as will be seen by the quarterly divisions of Statement of Earnings,
and your Directors feel encouraged with the progress made and look
for continued substantial improvement during 1913.
At a meeting of the Board of Directors held this day, a quarterly
dividend of one per cent was declared, out of surplus earnings, payable
April 10, 1913, to stockholders of record March 31, 1913.
By order of the BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
F. N. KONDOLF, President.
H. M. SADLER, Secretary.
New York, March 6, 1913.
