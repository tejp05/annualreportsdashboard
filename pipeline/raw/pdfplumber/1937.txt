To THE STOCKHOLDERS:
Your directors submit for the information of Stockholders
ConsoHdated Income and Surplus Account for the year
1937, together with Consolidated Balance Sheet as of
December 31, 1937, with the Auditors' Certificate attached.
Net Income for the year 1937, after deducting $737,021.00,
being the full amount of the Net Income and Undistributed
Net Profits for the year 1937 of foreign subsidiaries and
branches in countries where exchange is at present blocked
by governmental restrictions, but before deducting Federal
Taxes, was $10,412,512.80, compared with $9,127,956.41 for
the year 1936, being an increase of $1,284,556.39.
After deducting estimated Federal Tax;es of $2,330,000.00
(including $633,000.00'Surtax on Undistributed Profits) and
reserving in full for Blocked Foreign Net Profits of the year
amounting to $737,021.00, Net Income for 1937 was $8,082,-
512.80 compared with $7,552,956.41 for 1936. The Net In-
come for 1937 equals $10.42 per share on the 775,880 shares
outstanding December 31, 1937, compared with $9.73 for
the year 1936 if computed on the same number of shares,
being an increase of $.69 per share. A Stock Dividend of
36,946 shares was issued ia 1937, and the Net Income for

===== PAGE 6 =====
1936 on the 738,934 shares actually outstanding at the end
of that year was $10.22 per share.
Prior to the current year a reserve in the amount of
$1,414,838.00'was set aside out . of Surplus covering the
Blocked Foreign Net Profits for the years 1935 and 1936.
In addition to the $737,021.00 reserved out of 1937 Net
Income for Blocked Foreign Net Profits of that year, a
further reserve of $328,645.48 was set aside out of Surplus
to cover the full amount of Blocked Foreign Net Profits of
years prior to 1935 for which no reserve had previously been
made. Therefore all Blocked Foreign Net Profits are now
eliminated and covered 100% by Reserve.
The above Income includes a cash dividend of $150,000.00
received in December 1937 on the Company's holding of
100,000 shares of the Class "B" capital stock of the Hobart
Manufacturing Company.
Additions amounting to $10,609,415.13 were made during
the year for rental machines, plant and equipment and land
and buildings. Dismantled and obsolete equipment amount-
ing to $4,835,309.55 was written off and charged against
reserves provided out of prior and current years' earnings.
To provide additional working capital, the Company sold,
in December 1937, to the Prudential Insurance Company of
America $5,000,000.00 Ten-Year Sinking Fund Deben-
tures at par. These Debentures are redeemable through a
Sinking Fund at the rate of $400,000.00 annually commencing
December 15, 1942, or earlier at the Corporation's option.

Four quarterly cash dividends of $1.50 per share were paid
during the year. In addition, a stock dividend of 5% (38,794
shares) was declared in November 1937, deliverable April 1,
1938 or as soon thereafter as practicable to stockholders of
record March 15, 1938.
By order of the Board of Directors,
THOMAS J. WATSON,
President.

To the Stockholders of the
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION:
We have made an examination of the accounts and records of the
International Business Machines Corporation and its Domestic Sub-
sidiaries relating to the Consolidated Balance Sheet as at December 31,
1937 and reviewed the Consolidated Income and Surplus Account for
the year ended on that date, but we have not made a detailed audit of
all transactions. In that connection, we accepted after review an inde-
pendent Auditor's report on the accounts of the Canadian Corporation
and the Company's own financial statements on the accounts of the
other Foreign Subsidiaries and Branches. The Company's accounts have
been maintained consistently, except with respect to the elimination of
blocked foreign income and profits referred to in the footnote to the
accompanying Consolidated Income and Surplus Account, in accordance
v/ith accepted accounting principles, and all information and explana-
tions desired were furnished.
The accompanying Consolidated Balance Sheet, based upon such
examination, fairly presents, in our opinion, the financial condition of
the International Business Machines Corporation and its Domestic and
Canadian Subsidiary Companies as at December 31, 1937, and the re-
lated Consolidated Income and Surplus Account the results of opera-
tions for the year ended on that date.
R. G. RANKIN & CO.
Certified Public Accountants.
February 23, 1938.
