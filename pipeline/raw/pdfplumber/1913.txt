To the Stockholders of the
COMPUTING-TABULATING-RECORDING-COMPANY.
Herewith is submitted the Consolidated General Balance Sheet
of the Computing-Tabulating-Recording-Company and Subsidiary
Companies, as of December 31, 1913, also the Statement of Earnings
for the year 1913, certified by The Audit Company of New York.
The results from operations for the year, as will be seen by the
Statement of Earnings, show an increase in net earnings of $93,769.19
over the previous year, after deducting interest on bonds, all other
charges and expenses.
During the year $313,719.00 has been distributed to the stock-
holders in dividends, and $320,974.79 added to surplus. The
Company also retired $132,000 of its outstanding bonds.
The plants and property of the Company have been maintained in
good condition and adequate reserves have been set up for depreciation.
A large amount has been expended in new equipment and patents,
which should materially increase future earnings.
Your Directors feel satisfied with the progress made and look for
continued improvement during the year 1914.
By order of the BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
F. N. KONDOLF, President.
H. M. SADLER, Secretary.
New York, March 5, 1914.
