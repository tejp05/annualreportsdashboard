|_Revenue:_|Revenue is recognized from sales or sales-type leases when the product is shipped, from software when the program|

||in excess of six years. Costs to support or service licensed programs are charged against income as incurred or when|
||related revenue is recognized, whichever occurs first.|

||the straight-line method.|
