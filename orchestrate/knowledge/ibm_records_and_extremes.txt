IBM ALL-TIME RECORDS, PEAKS, AND FIRSTS (fiscal years 1911-2025)
Use this file to answer any 'peak', 'highest', 'record', 'best/worst year',
or 'first year over X' question. These superlatives are computed across the
ENTIRE verified series - do not infer peaks from individual year summaries.

REVENUE
- IBM's revenue PEAKED in FY2011 at $106,916 million ($106.9 billion). It has never been higher before or since.
- Top five revenue years: FY2011 $106,916 million ($106.9 billion); FY2012 $104,507 million ($104.5 billion); FY2008 $103,630 million ($103.6 billion); FY2010 $99,870 million ($99.9 billion); FY2013 $99,751 million ($99.8 billion).
- Revenue declined from the 2011 peak through 2020 (divestitures and portfolio shifts) and stepped down again with the Kyndryl managed-infrastructure spinoff (Nov 2021). Post-spinoff totals (FY2021 $57.4B -> FY2025 $67.5B) are a smaller revenue base by construction - FY2022's ~$60.5 billion is NOT an all-time peak, only a point on the post-Kyndryl series.
- First fiscal year revenue exceeded $100 million: FY1943 ($131 million).
- First fiscal year revenue exceeded $1 billion: FY1957 ($1,000 million ($1.0 billion)).
- First fiscal year revenue exceeded $10 billion: FY1973 ($10,993 million ($11.0 billion)).
- First fiscal year revenue exceeded $50 billion: FY1985 ($50,718 million ($50.7 billion)).
- First fiscal year revenue exceeded $100 billion: FY2008 ($103,630 million ($103.6 billion)).

NET INCOME AND LOSSES
- All-time highest net income: FY2012, $16,604 million ($16.6 billion).
- Worst year / largest loss: FY1993, $-8,101 million ($-8.1 billion) - at the time the largest annual loss in American corporate history (early-1990s crisis; losses ran 1991-1993, and Louis Gerstner arrived in 1993).
- First fiscal year net income exceeded $100 million: FY1958 ($126 million).
- First fiscal year net income exceeded $1 billion: FY1970 ($1,018 million ($1.0 billion)).
- First fiscal year net income exceeded $10 billion: FY2007 ($10,418 million ($10.4 billion)).
