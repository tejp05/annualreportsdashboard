IBM (AND CTR) LEADERSHIP, COMPANY ERAS, AND MILESTONES, 1911-2025
Cross-checked against chairman's-letter signatures in the annual reports.

COMPANY NAME
- 1911-1924: Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company (CTR)
- 1924-present: International Business Machines Corporation (IBM)

LEADERSHIP (CEO / CHAIRMAN SUCCESSION)
- George W. Fairchild, Chairman, 1911 to 1924.
- Thomas J. Watson Sr., President/CEO (Chairman from 1949), 1914 to 1956.
- Thomas J. Watson Jr., CEO/Chairman, 1956 to 1971.
- T. Vincent Learson, CEO/Chairman, 1971 to 1973.
- Frank T. Cary, CEO/Chairman, 1973 to 1981.
- John R. Opel, CEO/Chairman, 1981 to 1985.
- John F. Akers, CEO/Chairman, 1985 to 1993.
- Louis V. Gerstner Jr., CEO/Chairman, 1993 to 2002.
- Samuel J. Palmisano, CEO/Chairman, 2002 to 2011.
- Virginia M. Rometty, CEO/Chairman, 2012 to 2020.
- Arvind Krishna, CEO (Chairman from 2021), 2020 to present.

ERAS
- 1911-1923: CTR / formation
- 1924-1951: Tabulating machines & the Watson Sr. era
- 1952-1980: Mainframe ascent (701, System/360)
- 1981-1993: PC era & the early-'90s crisis
- 1993-2002: Gerstner services turnaround
- 2002-2018: Software & services, globalization
- 2019-2025: Hybrid cloud & AI (Red Hat, watsonx)

MILESTONES
- 1911: CTR incorporated from the merger of three firms
- 1914: Thomas J. Watson Sr. joins as general manager
- 1924: CTR renamed International Business Machines
- 1952: IBM 701, the company's first commercial scientific computer
- 1957: FORTRAN released
- 1964: System/360 mainframe family announced
- 1981: IBM Personal Computer (PC) launched
- 1993: Record loss; Louis Gerstner begins the services-led turnaround
- 2002: Acquires PwC Consulting; exits hard-disk business
- 2005: Sells PC division to Lenovo
- 2011: Watson wins Jeopardy!; 100th anniversary
- 2015: Forms Cognitive/Watson and Cloud units; 'strategic imperatives'
- 2019: Acquires Red Hat for ~$34B
- 2021: Spins off managed-infrastructure unit as Kyndryl
- 2023: watsonx AI and data platform launched
