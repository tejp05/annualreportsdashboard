IBM (and predecessor CTR) year-by-year financial summaries, FY1911-FY1955, extracted and hand-verified from the original annual reports. All dollar amounts are US$ millions unless written as billions.

Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company (CTR, IBM's predecessor) fiscal year 1911 (FY1911), from the 1911 annual report. All dollar figures are US$ millions as originally reported. Revenue: not reliably stated in the original filing (before 1940 CTR/IBM reported net earnings rather than a revenue figure). Net income: $0.951 million. Leadership: George W. Fairchild (Chairman). Era: CTR / formation. Milestone that year: CTR incorporated from the merger of three firms. Reporting-basis note: netIncome=net earnings of constituent companies, yr ended Apr 30 1911, before interest (CTR incorporated Jun 1911).
